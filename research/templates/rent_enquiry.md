# Local rent and operating-cost enquiry

Use for tasks of type `confirm_local_rent` and `collect_local_price`. Goal: **at least three observations per
location** (Pune, Nanded) for premises matching the business's space requirement, recorded in *Geography*.

## What to collect

| Metric | Segment to record | Acceptable evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial rent per sq ft per month | micro-market + frontage/floor (e.g. "Kothrud, high street, ground floor") | signed leave-and-licence figure shared by a tenant or owner (best); broker's written quote; landlord's asking rent |
| Industrial shed rent per sq ft per month | estate + shed size (e.g. "MIDC Bhosari, 2,000 sq ft") | MIDC or estate rate circular; broker's written quote |
| Security deposit (months of rent) | same segment | lease terms |
| Electricity energy charge per kWh | tariff category and slab | the current MERC tariff order (cite page) |
| Prevailing monthly wage | role (e.g. "machine operator, food unit") | three local employers; compare with the notified minimum wage |
| Local selling price | product, pack size, channel | shelf price observed in person (date, outlet type); distributor price list |

## Rules

- Record **asking** and **transacted** rents as separate observations and say which in the notes. Asking rents overstate.
- Property-listing websites may be consulted by a person but **not copied or scraped**; record what you personally
  confirmed with an owner, tenant or broker, with the date.
- Always record: carpet or built-up area basis, whether maintenance and property tax are included, lock-in period.
- A city figure never stands in for a district figure or the reverse. Attach each observation to the right node;
  the fallback resolver will show when a value is inherited from a higher level.
- A count of competitors or a population figure is an **indicator**. Do not convert it into a demand estimate.
- If you could not obtain three observations, record what you have and say so in the task — do not fill the gap.
