# Supplier quotation enquiry

Use for tasks of type `obtain_supplier_quotes`. Goal: **three written quotations from independent suppliers per
machine**, each for delivery to **Pune** and to **Nanded**, recorded in *Suppliers & quotes*.

A quotation only becomes evidence when the supplier has agreed to its use (`consent_to_use`). Ask explicitly.

## Before you write

1. Open the business record → *Costs & observations*. Note for the machine: name as printed in the source,
   quantity, and any capacity stated (kg/hour, units/day). The source's price is a benchmark to beat, not a target.
2. Identify suppliers yourself (manufacturer websites, trade associations, exhibitions, referrals from operating
   owners). **Do not copy listings or prices from B2B marketplaces** — their terms prohibit it (see the source registry).
3. Prefer one manufacturer, one authorised dealer and one supplier recommended by an operating owner.

## Message (email or letter)

> Subject: Request for quotation — {machine name}, {capacity}
>
> Dear {name},
>
> I am a researcher with PlanB, which studies the set-up costs of small businesses in Maharashtra. We are collecting
> current prices for the equipment below and would be grateful for a written quotation.
>
> **Item:** {machine name as you understand it}
> **Required capacity / specification:** {capacity, material of construction, power supply: single/three phase}
> **Quantity:** {n}
> **Delivery locations:** (a) Pune, (b) Nanded — please quote freight for each
>
> Please state separately:
> 1. Basic price per unit, and the model / specification it refers to
> 2. GST rate and amount
> 3. Packing, freight and transit insurance to each location
> 4. Installation and commissioning — included or extra (amount)
> 5. Accessories or spares needed to run the machine that are not in the basic price
> 6. Connected load (kW), floor space, and utilities required
> 7. Rated output per hour and what limits it in practice
> 8. Warranty period and what it covers; annual maintenance contract price
> 9. Delivery lead time and payment terms
> 10. Validity of this quotation
>
> **Use of your quotation.** With your permission we will record these prices in our research database and may show
> them, attributed or anonymised as you prefer, to people evaluating this business. Please tell us which you prefer,
> or if you would rather we did not use them at all. We will not share your contact details without separate consent.
>
> Thank you,
> {your name}, PlanB Research · {contact}

## Record in PlanB Intelligence

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Supplier | Legal name, type (manufacturer / authorised dealer / trader), city, GSTIN if given |
| Item, model/spec, capacity | Exactly as quoted |
| Quoted price | **Basic price per unit, in rupees** — not lakh |
| Tax treatment / rate | Exclusive or inclusive of GST, and the rate |
| Installation | Included / excluded |
| Freight terms | As quoted, per location |
| Quoted on / valid until | Dates on the quotation |
| Collection method | email / phone / in person / written quotation |
| Consent to use | Tick **only** if the supplier agreed in writing or on a recorded call; note how |
| Document | Upload the quotation PDF so the observation can reach grade A after review |

Phone-only prices are recorded as received but stay grade C until confirmed in writing.
A quotation is **not comparable** to another unless capacity, GST basis and installation basis match — say so in the notes.
